SPOONFUL — SALES AND PRICING POLICY (BUSINESS ACCOUNTS)
Document owner: Chief Revenue Officer, Tom Achterberg
Last updated: June 1, 2026

SCOPE

This policy governs how the Sales team prices and discounts business meal-kit contracts. Consumer subscription pricing is set by Marketing and is out of scope.

SALES REGIONS

Spoonful sells to business customers in four regions: North, South, East, and West. Each region has a Regional Sales Lead who owns the regional revenue target and approves discounts within their authority. Quarterly revenue targets are set per region by the Chief Revenue Officer at the start of each fiscal year.

PRODUCT LINES

Business customers can buy three products: Classic Box (standard meal kits), Family Box (larger portions for events), and Office Lunch (pre-portioned individual meals delivered to offices). Office Lunch has the highest margin and is the strategic priority for 2026.

DISCOUNT AUTHORITY

Sales representatives may offer discounts of up to 10% off list price without approval. Discounts from 10% to 20% require approval from the Regional Sales Lead. Discounts above 20% require approval from the Chief Revenue Officer and must include a written business justification. Multi-year contracts may receive an additional 5% discount, subject to the same approval thresholds.

QUARTERLY REVIEWS

At the end of each quarter, each Regional Sales Lead presents revenue, units sold, and discount levels for their region. Any region that misses its quarterly revenue target by more than 10% must present a recovery plan at the following leadership meeting. Regions are compared on revenue, units sold, and average discount rate.

CUSTOMER HANDOFF

When a business contract is signed, the Sales representative must hand the account to Customer Success within 2 business days, including notes on the customer's goals and any promises made during the sale. Sales representatives may not promise features, delivery windows, or refund terms that are not in the standard contract.
