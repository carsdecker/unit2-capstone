SPOONFUL PEOPLE TEAM — EMPLOYEE WELLBEING AND ENGAGEMENT POLICY
Document owner: Chief People Officer, Hannah Lindqvist
Last updated: April 8, 2026

PURPOSE

This policy describes how Spoonful measures employee satisfaction and the programmes we offer to support wellbeing, growth, and retention. It applies to all employees in every department: Engineering, Operations, Customer Care, Sales, Marketing, Culinary, and Finance.

MEASURING SATISFACTION

Spoonful runs an anonymous engagement survey twice a year, in March and September. Employees rate their overall satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5, along with questions on workload, management, growth, and compensation. Results are reported by department only when at least 5 employees in that department respond, to protect anonymity. Our target is an average satisfaction score of at least 4.0 out of 5 in every department.

INDUSTRY BENCHMARK

According to the external benchmarking provider Spoonful uses, the average employee satisfaction score for consumer technology and food delivery companies of our size is 3.7 out of 5. The benchmark for warehouse and fulfilment operations roles specifically is lower, at 3.4 out of 5. Spoonful aims to exceed the industry benchmark in every department.

FLEXIBLE WORK

Office-based employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, with core collaboration hours from 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Warehouse and Operations roles are site-based by nature; these employees receive shift preference scheduling, published at least 2 weeks in advance, and a shift-swap system managed by site supervisors.

TIME OFF

All employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave plus public holidays. Employees also receive 2 paid wellbeing days per year that can be taken at short notice without justification. After 5 years of continuous service, employees earn a 4-week paid sabbatical.

LEARNING AND GROWTH

Every employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, conferences, and certifications. Managers must hold a career development conversation with each direct report at least twice a year, separate from performance reviews.

ACTION PLANNING

After each engagement survey, any department scoring below 3.5 out of 5 must produce an action plan within 30 days, reviewed by the People team. The action plan names specific owners and is revisited at the next survey. Departments with high turnover, defined as more than 20% annual attrition, also receive a quarterly retention review.

RECOGNITION

Spoonful runs a peer recognition programme in which employees can nominate colleagues for monthly "Full Plate" awards. Each award includes a $100 gift card and a mention at the monthly all-hands meeting.

SUPPORT SERVICES

All employees and their dependants have free, confidential access to the Employee Assistance Programme, which provides counselling, financial advice, and legal guidance 24 hours a day.
